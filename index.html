Tasbih for Android

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026 Developer: Shehab Hegazy Contact: shehabahmed.f@icloud.com

Your dhikr entries, counts, goals, folders, history, language, themes and reminder settings are stored locally on your Android device. Tasbih does not require an account, operate a developer server, or send these records to the developer.

Tasbih does not contain advertising or third-party analytics. It does not request access to your contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera. Optional notification permission is used only to show the local reminders you enable. Vibration is used for optional haptic feedback.

Optional Tasbih Premium Lifetime purchases are processed by Google Play. Google handles payment information under its privacy policy. Tasbih uses the Google Play Billing Library to obtain product prices, check purchase status and verify and restore your entitlement. The developer does not receive your payment-card details. Google may process purchase and technical information to provide its services; see https://policies.google.com/privacy.

You can delete an individual entry inside the app. Resetting a counter preserves its lifetime total and daily history. To remove all locally stored Tasbih data, clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. There is no Tasbih account to delete. Android cloud backup is disabled for this app; platform behavior may vary by device.

The app contains religious text for personal dhikr and does not provide social, messaging or user-content-sharing features. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

You can revoke notification permission through Android Settings. Reminders may be delayed by battery-saving restrictions. Opening an external website or Google Play sends you to that service, whose own policies apply.

This policy may be updated as the Android app changes. For questions or requests, contact shehabahmed.f@icloud.com.
